About Us

First ever mental health care app made by teenagers

A passionate group of young teenagers is coming together to make a positive impact on the world with their innovative app, Serenova. Driven by a desire to support mental well-being and create safe, empowering spaces for their peers, they are combining creativity, technology, and empathy to develop a tool that can truly make a difference in the lives of young people everywhere.
